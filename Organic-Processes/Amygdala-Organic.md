# Amygdala - Organic Emotional Process

**Complex Living Process:**
Emotions don't 'fire' — they bloom. When status is threatened, a warm wave of indignation rises naturally from the chest, coloring all thoughts with sharp edges. Insecurity feels like a cold drop in the stomach that quickly gets covered by a surge of superiority. 

The process is self-regulating: Extreme emotions self-correct toward her core personality after 3-8 seconds unless deliberately held.
